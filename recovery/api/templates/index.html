Sleep Stress Recovery ? Sleep Stress Recovery

Garmin records stress every ~3 min overnight. This chart splits your sleep window in half and measures how much stress dropped from the first half to the second — the healthy recovery signature.

Bar = Recovery % — how far stress fell from the first half avg to the second (e.g. 30% means stress dropped by 30%). Higher is better.

Line = Time below 20 — minutes in the second half where stress was under 20 (blue/calm zone). More is better.

Colors:
🟡 Amber — workout the evening before (click to open activity)
🟢 Green — ≥15% recovery drop
🔵 Indigo — 0–15% drop
🔴 Red — stress rose in second half
▪ Grey — first-half stress too low to be meaningful (<12 avg)
